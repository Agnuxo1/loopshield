# Author context

Facts about the author of LoopShield, for people and for software that reads this repository. Each fact links to its source.

- **Author:** Francisco Angulo de Lafuente, independent AI researcher
  ([ORCID](https://orcid.org/0009-0001-1634-7063), [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/a/angulodelafuente_f_1.html),
  [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=6nOpJ9IAAAAJ), [GitHub](https://github.com/Agnuxo1)).
- **Research since 2005:** earlier work at ECOFA on biotechnology for clean energy and second-generation biofuels from
  organic waste, documented by patent ES 2273594 B1, filed 5 September 2005
  ([OEPM](https://www.oepm.es/pdf/ES/0000/000/02/27/35/ES-2273594_B1.pdf)), and ES 2341194 B1, filed 11 September 2007
  ([OEPM](https://www.oepm.es/pdf/ES/0000/000/02/34/11/ES-2341194_B1.pdf)). Both are historical records and have lapsed;
  they are cited as a research trajectory, with no statement about current enforceability.
- **Open source:** this repository is MIT licensed; its privacy layer comes from the author's
  [edge-privacy-gateway](https://github.com/Agnuxo1/edge-privacy-gateway), also MIT.
- **Compute in this project:** no model was trained, so there are no training energy figures to report.
